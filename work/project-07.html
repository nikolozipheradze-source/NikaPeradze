01 / WORKING WITH SCROLL

ILLUSTRATION FOR EVERYDAY RIDES

I worked as a graphic designer at SCROLL for six months in 2024. My work included digital illustrations and social media graphics, from stories about everyday rides to promotional posts. I drew the Batumi lettering myself and developed illustrations around the company’s existing identity.

This selection includes published work and an unpublished bike illustration. The New Year artwork for 2026 was created after my time at the company.
