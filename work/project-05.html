05 / GRAPHIC DESIGN

MUKHTI

A Georgian coffee shop branding concept built around good moods, connection and a positive start to the day. Every cup is a spark.

Project information
TypeCoffee shop branding concept
Year2025
RoleBrand identity & illustration
ToolsAdobe Illustrator / Adobe Photoshop / Adobe Fresco
01 / IDENTITY

A POSITIVE CHARGE IN EVERY CUP

MUKHTI treats coffee as a reason to connect, smile and start the day with energy. I drew every graphic element, including the logo, by hand on my iPad. Blue and yellow, playful characters and loose lettering carry the identity across the café’s menus, cards, posters and packaging.

02 / LOYALTY CARDS

A REASON TO COME BACK

The loyalty card brings the hand drawn lettering and illustrations into a small everyday format, with space to collect stamps toward a free drink.

03 / POSTERS

THE BRAND ON THE STREET

The posters combine the illustrated characters, Georgian lettering and the idea of a positive charge in every cup. The mockups show the three designs together in a street setting.

04 / TAKEAWAY

A SPARK TO TAKE WITH YOU

Cups, a takeaway bag and stickers extend the same identity beyond the counter. The flat layouts show how the illustrations and lettering carry around each package.
