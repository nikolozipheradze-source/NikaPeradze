04 / GRAPHIC DESIGN

LUKMA

CHOCOLATE BRAND IDENTITY & PACKAGING

LUKMA is a chocolate packaging concept inspired by Georgian food culture. Its name means “bite” in Georgian, and its four flavors bring honey, walnut, pomegranate and red wine into one visual family.

Project information
TypeBrand identity & packaging
Year2025
RoleConcept & graphic design
ToolsInDesign / Photoshop / Illustrator
01 / CONCEPT

FLAVORS WITH A SENSE OF PLACE

The idea began with ingredients and flavors connected to Georgian food culture. Honey, walnut, pomegranate and red wine give each chocolate its own character and a clear starting point for its illustrations.

I wanted the range to feel expressive and recognizable without losing the connection between the four packages. The workbook traces the references, color direction and decisions behind the final designs.

Moodboard from the workbook. Open the page to see it at full size.

02 / IDENTITY

ONE MARK, FOUR PERSONALITIES

The cacao pod became LUKMA’s mark. I paired it with strong type and detailed ingredient illustrations, then changed the patterns and colors for each flavor. Honey is deep blue, walnut is dark and earthy, and the fruit flavors use warmer tones.

The logo and label keep the same position across the range, making the four designs read as one brand even when the illustrations change.

The cacao pod mark and typography from the workbook.

03 / PACKAGING

FROM LAYOUT TO PACKAGE

I planned the package panels before applying the final graphics. The artwork wraps around each box, with the flavor name on the front and product information on the reverse. The two layouts below show how the shared structure adapts to different ingredients.

Early package structure from the workbook.

04 / FINAL DESIGN

FOUR FLAVORS, TWO FORMATS

The final digital mockups show the same brand language across the four 40 g boxes and 200 g chocolate bars. Each flavor has its own illustrated pattern and color, while the mark and label remain consistent.

05 / LARGER BARS

THE LARGER FORMAT

The ingredient patterns also carry across the longer wrappers. Shown together, the four bars complete the collection.
